# NPC Generator Cheat Sheet

When you need to generate a NPC rapidly, you can use this table to guide you. Either select the *Threat* level this NPC could pose to your players' group or select a line that corresponds to the highest level among your players' characters.

This will determine pools of points to use on *Abilities*, *Focuses*, *Talents*, and *Specializations*. See [Option 2: Buying Abilities](ter-02-character-creation#212-step-2-abilities) in Chapter 2 Character Creation.
