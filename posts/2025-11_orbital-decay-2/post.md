[Here](orbital-decay-ter-tests.md) are most of the tests in this scenario, transposed into what I think are suitable TER equivalent. ### Hazards

In this scenario, apart from the nasty bugs infecting the station, there are three main hazards:

- **Explosions** in Green Sector (p. 17): As stated in the **Hazard Category** table in the **Hazards** section of Chapter 5 in 'The Expanse' rulebook (p. 100), an exploding panel could easily inflict **3d6** of damage. Depending on what you think would qualify as 'small explosions', feel free to lessen the damage down to **2d6** or even **1d6** (non-penetrating).
- **Fire** in Green Sector (p. 17): For simplicity's sake, consider using the same rule as for explosions, with vac suits acting as protection for short exposure (1-2 rounds).
- **Decompression** (p. 17): Refer to the **Explosive Decompression** section of Chapter 2 in the 'Ships Of The Expanse' rulebook (p. 42). For hard vacuum exposure, refer to the **Common Hazards** section of Chapter 13 in 'The Expanse' rulebook (p. 207).
